Collector & Styling Insights

Frontier & Western Aesthetic

The Western Throw Pillows collection draws direct inspiration from historic frontier iconography — weathered leather tones, dusty plains, sheriff star insignia, and the timeless spirit of cowboy life. Each piece blends classic Americana with crisp digital illustration.

Archival Materials & Fade Resistance

Home decor items utilize high-density spun polyester and soft microfiber with full edge-to-edge dye sublimation. Colors penetrate deeply into the fibers for rich contrast that resists sunlight fading and everyday wear.

Interior Styling Recommendations

Pair these focal pieces with warm neutrals, textured wood or metal accents, and complementary lighting. Mixing framed prints with accent pillows or tapestries creates layered visual depth in bedrooms, living rooms, and offices.

Available through Redbubble in multiple sizes, from small accent pillows to large floor

pillows. The covers feature vivid, fade-resistant prints and easy-zip removal for washing.

A perfect addition to any western, ranch, or rustic-themed interior.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are items in the Western Throw Pillows collection printed and fulfilled?

Every item in Pieter's Western Throw Pillows collection is printed on demand using state-of-the-art Direct-to-Garment (DTG) or dye-sublimation printing through Redbubble and TeePublic. This ensures sharp, rich colors, intricate linework, and archival inks that resist fading and washing wear.

What sizes and materials are used for western throw pillows?

Throw pillows are available with double-sided original art prints on 100% spun polyester poplin fabric. Sizes typically range from 16x16 inches up to 20x20 inches, as well as oversized floor pillow options. They feature concealed zippers and resilient polyester inserts.

Are the throw pillow covers machine washable?

Yes. The pillow covers can be unzipped, removed from the insert, and machine washed in cold water on a gentle cycle. Air dry or tumble dry low for best longevity.

Where are orders shipped, and how long does delivery take?

Orders are produced and dispatched from regional fulfillment centers across North America, Europe, Australia, and the UK to minimize shipping times. Production typically takes 2–4 business days, followed by standard or expedited tracked shipping.

Are all designs original works by Pieter?

Yes! Every single artwork featured across Pieter's POD Art is an authentic original creation. Purchasing through the official Redbubble and TeePublic links ensures you receive genuine authorized prints while directly supporting the artist.
