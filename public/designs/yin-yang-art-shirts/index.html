Frequently Asked Questions

How are items in the Yin Yang Art Shirts collection printed and fulfilled?

Every item in Pieter's Yin Yang Art Shirts collection is printed on demand using state-of-the-art Direct-to-Garment (DTG) or dye-sublimation printing through Redbubble and TeePublic. This ensures sharp, rich colors, intricate linework, and archival inks that resist fading and washing wear.

What sizes and apparel materials are available?

Apparel items are available in standard unisex, men's, and women's cuts ranging from Small to 5XL (depending on garment style). T-shirts are crafted from 100% pre-shrunk ring-spun cotton or ultra-soft cotton-poly blends. Check the sizing guide on the product page for exact chest and length measurements.

How should I wash and care for graphic tees and leggings?

To preserve vibrant print colors, machine wash garments inside-out in cold water with similar colors. Use mild detergent and tumble dry on low or hang dry. Avoid ironing directly on the printed design areas.

Where are orders shipped, and how long does delivery take?

Orders are produced and dispatched from regional fulfillment centers across North America, Europe, Australia, and the UK to minimize shipping times. Production typically takes 2–4 business days, followed by standard or expedited tracked shipping.

Are all designs original works by Pieter?

Yes! Every single artwork featured across Pieter's POD Art is an authentic original creation. Purchasing through the official Redbubble and TeePublic links ensures you receive genuine authorized prints while directly supporting the artist.
